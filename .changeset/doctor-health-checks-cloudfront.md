`posterna doctor` points at the two setups that most often break a working install. When the signature headers do not reach the app, its fix names CloudFront's origin request policy (`AllViewerExceptHostHeader`), which CloudFront needs before it passes them, the real User-Agent and Accept-Language on. When the proxy guard has suspended enforcement, its fix says that load balancer health checks can cause it on a quiet site, and links to the new install guide section on keeping them out of Posterna.
